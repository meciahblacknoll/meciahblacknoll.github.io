Overhead-projector 4K conversion

A dead overhead projector already carries most of the optics this build needs — a Fresnel stage and a focused periscope lens. The conversion swaps the lamp for an LED and the transparency sheet for a disassembled phone's LCD panel. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom.

Stage/glass/Fresnel sourced from 3M's 9000-series parts manual; head & arm split still estimated. drag · scroll · OpenSCAD source linked below
    Why this works: most OHPs collimate lamp light through a built-in Fresnel lens, then route it through a transparency and up into a periscope head holding a mirror + projection lens already focused for a normal-room throw. Replacing the lamp with a brighter LED and the transparency with a phone's LCD panel (backlight pulled) reuses that entire optical path — no Fresnel or projection lens needs to be sourced separately.
