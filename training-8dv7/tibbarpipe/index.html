What changed from the first model

Black steel pipe, not PVC. Threaded fittings, not a drilled carriage bolt. A tripod base for stability instead of resting flat on a foot. No hinge anywhere in the bar itself — a build like this is a rigid lever, balanced across a separate fixed point (a block, a step, a low box) when it's actually used, the way a real seesaw works, rather than pivoting on a built-in joint.

One reported build of this design ran about $42 in modified parts; the plain 3/4" version in the list above should land under that.
