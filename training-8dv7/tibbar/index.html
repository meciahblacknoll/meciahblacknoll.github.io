Parts

What the bar is made of

1
1" PVC pipe, cut to 12–15 inches — the lever itself.
2
3/8" carriage bolt, 4–5" long, through a hole drilled about 2" from one end — this becomes the plate hanger.
3
2 washers + 1 nut + 1 wing nut — nut locks the bolt in the pipe; washer + wing nut hold plates on the hanging side, removable by hand.
4
Foam pipe insulation or a towel + tape — wrapped on the opposite end, where the top of the foot rests.
5
Weight plates you already own — the load. Start with the smallest one you have.
< $10pipe + bolt + hardware, hardware-store prices

Build order

Five steps

  1. Cut the PVC pipe to length (12–15"). Sand or file the cut edges smooth.
  2. Measure 2" in from one end. Drill straight through the pipe wall, both sides, sized to the carriage bolt's shank.
  3. Push the bolt through so its threaded end sticks out one side. Thread a washer and nut onto the far side, snug, so the bolt can't spin or slide in the hole.
  4. Wrap the opposite end — the last 4–5 inches — in foam pipe insulation, or several layers of towel held on with tape, until it's comfortable to rest the top of a bare or socked foot on.
  5. Slide a plate onto the exposed bolt shank, add a washer, thread on the wing nut. That's the loaded end; the padded end is the foot end.
Before the first real set: hang one light plate, sit on the bench, rest the padded end on your foot, and do one slow rep. Check that the pipe doesn't roll off the top of your foot and that the wing-nut end clears the floor at the bottom of the lowering phase. That's the fit check the training doc asks for in week 1.
