Reading the model

  • The teal dot is the only thing that doesn't move. That's the heel. Everything else — foot, pipe, bolt, plates — rotates together around it as one rigid piece during the rep.
  • The pipe is drawn at 14 inches, inside the 12–15" range, 1" diameter. The bolt sits 2" from the loaded end, matching the build notes.
  • At rest (slider at 0) the toes point forward, plate end low. At full dorsiflexion (slider at 100) the toes have pulled up toward the shin by about 20°, which is what lifts the plates — that lift is the resistance you're working against.
  • Orbit underneath or from the side to confirm the bolt's shank clears the floor at the bottom of the lowering phase, which is the fit check the training doc asks for in week 1.
