Rev G tray: your queen sits inside the 63.5 × 83.5 in box on 2x6 sleepers and 1x6 slats, with 3 in of rail standing proud as a rim; one 7/16 OSB bottom sheet is both floor and face. Two 12 V linear actuators lift it. Bed down reaches 102.5 in from the rear wall and leaves 51.5 in to the entry wall. Standing it is 86 in tall and 25 in deep. The swing's highest point (86 in) is 21 in from the rear wall; under the fan it passes 18 in below the light glass (86 in) and 12 in below the 20 in blade tips. No headboard; the rails are the rim. Door and window positions are Meciah's tape measurements of 15 Nov 2026; the window's height is still assumed. Squat rack: a gate-style fold-flat rack on the right wall between the closet door and the window: two 4x4 posts on 21 in 2x6 arms that swing on 1/2 in hinge bolts from two 2x6 stringers lagged to the studs. Folded it is 5 in off the wall; out, the bar sits 28 in off the wall and 102 in from the rear wall, clear of the standing bed and the window. Use it with the bed up only. Cable: one DIY setup is drawn, a lat pulldown: pulley 1 under the pull-up pipe, pulley 2 on a bracket off the top stringer, the loading pin and plates hanging against the wall, your flat bench inside the rack. In cable mode the safety drops to the 26 in pins and wears a length of pipe insulation: that is the knee hold-down, like the pad on a gym pulldown. The loading pin rests on the floor between sets so plates go on standing up. Move pulley 2 to the bottom arm pin for a low row. Blue comforter and blue blackout curtains added; the rack stringers stop at 104 in so the stacked curtain clears them. What saves you: spotter arms, not the plank. Each post carries a pair of 2x8 cheeks, one on each face, bolted through two of its holes with the bolts in double shear and projecting 24 in, plus a 2x4 leg from the arm tip to a foot at the post base, so a dropped bar goes arm, leg, floor. Gate locks are vertical drop pins top and bottom. Pull-up pipe is 1-1/2 in schedule 40, pinned in a 2 in notch. These are the fixes from the 23 Nov independent review. Press "Fail a squat" or "Fail a bench" to watch the bar come off the J-blocks, move out in front of the posts and land on the arms. Storage, rack folded: plates on a 14 in peg under the window, bar and pull-up pipe upright in the rear-right corner in a floor cup with a clip at 78 in, the plank on two hooks above the top stringer. The spotter arms, legs and feet come off on four pins and hang on a second pair of hooks above the plank. The bench (44 x 10 in, 17 in high) hangs upright on two hooks on the left wall at 31 to 41 in from the entry wall, legs to the wall, 12 in off it, clear of the entry door's swing. The bookshelf is gone. The TV is gone: the DIY projector sits on a shelf at 96 in on the rear wall above the standing bed and throws onto an 80 x 45 in screen painted across the closet doors. Closet wall B (chosen): the entry wall past the door casing carries a 20 in deep, 92.5 in wide, floor-to-ceiling plywood carcass, four 23 in columns in two rows, eight bays; each bay has a 20 in rod running out from the wall on 24 in full-extension slides with hangers at 2 in pitch facing you, about 72 garments, and the bay's door face rides out with its rod.
