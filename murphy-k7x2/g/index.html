Rev G tray: your queen sits inside the 63.5 × 83.5 in box on 2x6 sleepers and 1x6 slats, with 3 in of rail standing proud as a rim; one 7/16 OSB bottom sheet is both floor and face. Two 12 V linear actuators lift it. Bed down reaches 102.5 in from the rear wall and leaves 51.5 in to the entry wall. Standing it is 86 in tall and 25 in deep. The swing's highest point (86 in) is 21 in from the rear wall; under the fan it passes 18 in below the light glass (86 in) and 12 in below the 20 in blade tips. No headboard; the rails are the rim. Door and window positions are Meciah's tape measurements of 15 Nov 2026; the window's height is still assumed. Squat rack: a gate-style fold-flat rack on the right wall between the closet door and the window: two 4x4 posts on 21 in 2x6 arms that swing on 1/2 in hinge bolts from two 2x6 stringers lagged to the studs. Folded it is 5 in off the wall; out, the bar sits 28 in off the wall and 102 in from the rear wall, clear of the standing bed and the window. Use it with the bed up only. Cable: one DIY setup is drawn, a lat pulldown: pulley 1 under the pull-up pipe, pulley 2 on a bracket off the top stringer, the loading pin and plates hanging against the wall, your flat bench inside the rack. In cable mode the safety drops to the 26 in pins and wears a length of pipe insulation: that is the knee hold-down, like the pad on a gym pulldown. The loading pin rests on the floor between sets so plates go on standing up. Move pulley 2 to the bottom arm pin for a low row. Blue comforter and blue blackout curtains added; the rack stringers stop at 104 in so the stacked curtain clears them. What saves you: spotter arms, not the plank. Each post carries a 2x6 arm bolted through two of its holes, with a 2x4 leg down to a 2x4 foot at the post base, so a dropped bar goes arm, leg, floor. Press "Fail a squat" or "Fail a bench" to watch the bar come off the J-blocks, move out in front of the posts and land on the arms. Storage, rack folded: plates on a 14 in peg under the window, bar and pull-up pipe upright in the rear-right corner in a floor cup with a clip at 78 in, the plank on two hooks above the top stringer. The spotter arms, legs and feet fold with the posts.
