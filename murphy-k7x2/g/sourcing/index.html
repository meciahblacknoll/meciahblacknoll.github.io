7. Not worth it

  • Craigslist OKC "Linear actuators" $125: two 2,000 lb units, no stroke stated, posted 15 Oct. Ours need 18 in of stroke and $104 buys two new ones with a return window.
  • Craigslist "reclaimed lumber" $150 in Stratford: three trailer loads of random wood, an hour away.
  • Cedar fence panels at $35 to $45 each: pickets alone cost $2.
  • Craigslist "12V DC power supply" listings at $25 to $90: a free ATX computer supply does this job.
